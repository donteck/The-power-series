## Production Pipeline

Each Power Series title follows this production sequence:

1. Select the motivational word.
2. Convert each letter into a principle.
3. Build one module per letter.
4. Create a Roadmaps prompt under 400 characters.
5. Expand modules into lessons and chapters.
6. Develop subtitle and book positioning.
7. Create subject-specific visual identity and cover.
8. Write front and back matter.
9. Prepare Amazon description, YouTube description, and keywords.
10. Package roadmap, course, manuscript, and marketing assets.

## Planned Expansion

Future words may include Excellence, Vision, Growth, Persistence, Determination, Freedom, and Legacy.

New titles should avoid repeating a previously used core word unless intentionally creating an edition, workbook, or companion volume.

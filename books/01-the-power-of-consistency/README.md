# The Power of Consistency

**Subtitle:** The 11 Principles to Build Unbreakable Discipline, Achieve Extraordinary Results, and Become Your Best Self

**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Framework

1. C — Commitment: Stay Dedicated
2. O — Ownership: Take Responsibility
3. N — No Excuses: Find Solutions
4. S — Self-Discipline: Take Action
5. I — Intention: Act with Purpose
6. S — Sacrifice: Trade Comfort for Growth
7. T — Tenacity: Persist
8. E — Endurance: Stay Strong
9. N — Never Quit
10. C — Continuous Improvement
11. Y — Your Why

## Roadmaps Prompt

Build CONSISTENCY as 11 modules, one per letter: C—Commitment: stay dedicated. O—Ownership: take responsibility. N—No Excuses: find solutions. S—Self-Discipline: take action. I—Intention: act with purpose. S—Sacrifice: trade comfort for growth. T—Tenacity: persist. E—Endurance: stay strong. N—Never Quit. C—Continuous Improvement. Y—Your Why.

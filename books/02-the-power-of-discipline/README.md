# The Power of Discipline

**Subtitle:** The 10 Principles to Master Yourself, Build Powerful Habits, and Turn Your Goals into Results

**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Framework

1. D — Determination: Finish Strong
2. I — Integrity: Keep Promises
3. S — Self-Control: Master Impulses
4. C — Commitment: Stay Dedicated
5. I — Intention: Act Purposely
6. P — Persistence: Keep Going
7. L — Laser Focus: Avoid Distractions
8. I — Improvement: Grow Daily
9. N — Non-Negotiables: Keep Standards
10. E — Execution: Take Action

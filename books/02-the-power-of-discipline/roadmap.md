# The Power of Discipline — Roadmap

**Subtitle:** The 10 Principles to Master Yourself, Build Powerful Habits, and Turn Your Goals into Results  
**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Framework

DISCIPLINE = 10 principles and 10 modules.

1. **D — Determination:** Finish Strong
2. **I — Integrity:** Keep Promises
3. **S — Self-Control:** Master Impulses
4. **C — Commitment:** Stay Dedicated
5. **I — Intention:** Act Purposely
6. **P — Persistence:** Keep Going
7. **L — Laser Focus:** Avoid Distractions
8. **I — Improvement:** Grow Daily
9. **N — Non-Negotiables:** Keep Standards
10. **E — Execution:** Take Action

## Module 1 — Determination
- Understand the psychology of the finish line.
- Recognize and overcome the mid-project slump.
- Build grit when motivation declines.
- Strengthen mental toughness under pressure.
- Create a finish-strong strategy.

## Module 2 — Integrity
- Understand how self-trust strengthens discipline.
- Identify the cost of unkept promises.
- Treat your word as a personal commitment.
- Repair broken commitments without self-deception.
- Build a reputation for reliability with yourself and others.

## Module 3 — Self-Control
- Understand the neuroscience of immediate gratification.
- Identify triggers, temptations, and recurring weaknesses.
- Practice the pause principle before reacting.
- Add friction to destructive habits.
- Increase your tolerance for discomfort.

## Module 4 — Commitment
- Define your values and non-negotiable mission.
- Build commitment that survives changing motivation.
- Use the principle of burning unnecessary escape routes.
- Connect present effort to long-term vision.
- Re-sign your personal commitment contract regularly.

## Module 5 — Intention
- Distinguish reactive living from intentional living.
- Connect daily tasks to meaningful goals.
- Eliminate busywork that creates movement without progress.
- Build a purpose-driven agenda.
- Recognize the true cost of your time.

## Module 6 — Persistence
- Treat failure as data rather than identity.
- Build resilience after disappointment.
- Practice micro-persistence through small repeated actions.
- Manage fatigue and burnout intelligently.
- Continue through plateaus without abandoning the goal.

## Module 7 — Laser Focus
- Understand cognitive load and attention limits.
- Conduct a personal distraction audit.
- Build deep-work rituals.
- Protect your attention from interruptions.
- Train yourself to return attention quickly after disruption.

## Module 8 — Improvement
- Apply Kaizen and daily incremental growth.
- Use reflection to improve performance.
- Identify blind spots and weak points.
- Stack complementary skills.
- Build a feedback loop that converts experience into growth.

## Module 9 — Non-Negotiables
- Create a personal code.
- Establish standards before pressure arrives.
- Use boundaries to protect priorities.
- Understand the cost of repeatedly lowering the bar.
- Automate important routines wherever possible.

## Module 10 — Execution
- Distinguish planning from doing.
- Defeat analysis paralysis and perfectionism.
- Use immediate-action rules to reduce hesitation.
- Batch and sequence work for efficient execution.
- Build a daily execution system.

## Roadmaps Prompt
Build DISCIPLINE as 10 modules, one per letter: D—Determination: finish strong. I—Integrity: keep promises. S—Self-Control: master impulses. C—Commitment: stay dedicated. I—Intention: act purposely.

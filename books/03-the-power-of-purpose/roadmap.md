# The Power of Purpose — Roadmap

**Subtitle:** The 7 Principles to Discover Your Direction, Live with Intention, and Build a Life That Matters  
**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Framework

PURPOSE = 7 principles and 7 modules.

1. **P — Passion:** Discover What Drives You
2. **U — Understanding:** Know Yourself Deeply
3. **R — Responsibility:** Own Your Direction
4. **P — Priorities:** Focus on What Matters
5. **O — Opportunity:** Recognize Possibilities
6. **S — Service:** Create Value for Others
7. **E — Execution:** Turn Purpose into Action

## Module 1 — Passion
- Identify core interests and moments of flow.
- Distinguish temporary excitement from lasting drive.
- Examine activities that consistently increase your energy.
- Revisit childhood curiosity, talents, and recurring interests.
- Build a personal passion inventory.

## Module 2 — Understanding
- Clarify values and beliefs.
- Study past triumphs for patterns of strength.
- Identify resistance and limiting beliefs.
- Assess strengths, capabilities, and growth areas.
- Create an honest identity profile.

## Module 3 — Responsibility
- Understand locus of control and accountability.
- Recognize the cost of inaction.
- Replace victim mentality with ownership.
- Establish boundaries around your direction.
- Write a manifesto of personal responsibility.

## Module 4 — Priorities
- Use prioritization tools such as the Eisenhower Matrix.
- Filter distractions through your values.
- Design an ideal weekly allocation of time and energy.
- Practice the strategic no.
- Conduct a recurring commitment audit.

## Module 5 — Opportunity
- Develop an abundance and possibility mindset.
- Look for intersections between trends and personal strengths.
- Treat obstacles as data and potential open doors.
- Make room for useful serendipity.
- Run purpose-driven experiments before making major commitments.

## Module 6 — Service
- Connect your skills with real needs.
- Identify the people or communities your work can benefit.
- Measure success through contribution as well as achievement.
- Practice daily service in practical ways.
- Understand the ripple effect of useful work.

## Module 7 — Execution
- Convert purpose into 90-day goals.
- Build daily habits that support your direction.
- Use accountability to maintain momentum.
- Address procrastination directly.
- Review and refine your purpose roadmap as you grow.

## Roadmaps Prompt
Build PURPOSE as 7 modules, one per letter: P—Passion: discover what drives you. U—Understanding: know yourself deeply. R—Responsibility: own your direction. P—Priorities: focus on what matters. O—Opportunity: recognize possibilities. S—Service: create value for others. E—Execution: turn purpose into action.

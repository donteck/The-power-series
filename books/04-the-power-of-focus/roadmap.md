# The Power of Focus — Roadmap

**Subtitle:** The 5 Principles to Master Your Attention, Eliminate Distractions, and Achieve What Matters Most  
**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Framework

FOCUS = 5 principles and 5 modules.

1. **F — Filter:** Eliminate Distractions
2. **O — Objectives:** Define What Matters Most
3. **C — Concentration:** Master Your Attention
4. **U — Uninterrupted Action:** Protect Deep Work
5. **S — Single-Minded Execution:** Finish What You Start

## Module 1 — Filter
- Audit your digital and physical environment.
- Identify internal triggers and mental clutter.
- Apply digital minimalism to reduce unnecessary inputs.
- Redesign notifications and communication habits.
- Build a distraction-resistant workspace.

## Module 2 — Objectives
- Create clarity around goals and priorities.
- Apply the Pareto principle to identify high-value work.
- Use the One Thing principle to focus effort.
- Align short-term objectives with long-term direction.
- Create a values-based priority filter.

## Module 3 — Concentration
- Understand the neuroscience of attention.
- Recognize the cost of task-switching and cognitive fatigue.
- Use mindfulness and attention-training exercises.
- Work with natural energy rhythms.
- Build a disciplined single-tasking practice.

## Module 4 — Uninterrupted Action
- Design a repeatable deep-work ritual.
- Use time-blocking to protect important work.
- Establish boundaries around interruptions.
- Create buffers for communication and unexpected demands.
- Develop a transition ritual that helps you enter flow.

## Module 5 — Single-Minded Execution
- Identify procrastination and fear of completion.
- Break large projects into executable units.
- Practice done-over-perfect when perfectionism stalls progress.
- Use weekly reviews to protect momentum.
- Build accountability loops that increase completion.

## Roadmaps Prompt
Build FOCUS as 5 modules, one per letter: F—Filter: eliminate distractions. O—Objectives: define what matters most. C—Concentration: master your attention. U—Uninterrupted Action: protect deep work. S—Single-Minded Execution: finish what you start.

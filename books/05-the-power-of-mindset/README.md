# The Power of Mindset

**Subtitle:** The 7 Principles to Transform Your Thinking, Strengthen Your Beliefs, and Create the Life You Envision

**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Framework

1. M — Mentality: Master Your Thinking
2. I — Identity: Define Who You Are Becoming
3. N — Narrative: Rewrite Your Inner Story
4. D — Development: Embrace Growth
5. S — Self-Belief: Trust Your Potential
6. E — Empowerment: Take Control of Your Choices
7. T — Transformation: Turn New Thinking into a New Life

## Roadmaps Prompt

Build MINDSET as 7 modules, one per letter: M—Mentality: master your thinking. I—Identity: define who you are becoming. N—Narrative: rewrite your inner story. D—Development: embrace growth. S—Self-Belief: trust your potential. E—Empowerment: take control of your choices. T—Transformation: turn new thinking into a new life.

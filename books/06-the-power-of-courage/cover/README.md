# Cover Workspace — The Power of Courage

**Title:** The Power of Courage  
**Subtitle:** The 7 Principles to Face Fear, Take Bold Action, and Become Stronger Through Every Challenge  
**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Visual Direction

Primary identity: deep crimson/red, charcoal, warm sunrise gold, and strong high-contrast typography.

Central metaphor: a lone figure moving across a dramatic bridge or standing on a mountain summit, representing the decision to move forward despite fear and uncertainty.

The cover should visually reinforce the seven COURAGE principles:
Conviction, Ownership, Uncertainty, Resilience, Action, Growth, and Endurance.

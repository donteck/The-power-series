## Core Philosophy

Patience is not passive waiting. It is disciplined preparation for the right moment: the ability to control urgency, tolerate uncertainty, protect judgment, continue meaningful action, and recognize that some outcomes require preparation, repetition, timing, and maturity before they can be sustained.

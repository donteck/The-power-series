## PATIENCE Framework

- **P — Perspective:** See Beyond the Moment
- **A — Acceptance:** Stop Fighting What You Cannot Control
- **T — Timing:** Respect the Right Moment
- **I — Inner Calm:** Stay Grounded Under Pressure
- **E — Endurance:** Keep Going Without Forcing
- **N — Non-Reactivity:** Respond Instead of React
- **C — Consistency:** Keep Showing Up
- **E — Expectation:** Trust the Process Without Entitlement

## Core Philosophy

Patience is not passive waiting. It is disciplined steadiness: the ability to control urgency, tolerate uncertainty, protect judgment, continue meaningful action, and recognize that some outcomes require preparation, repetition, timing, and maturity before they can be sustained.

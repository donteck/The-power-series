## Core Philosophy

Information alone is not power. Knowledge becomes powerful when information is understood, organized, tested, connected to experience, evaluated with discernment, and translated into intelligent action. This book teaches readers how to build a personal knowledge system that improves judgment, capability, leadership, business performance, and long-term growth.

# The Power of Knowledge — Cover

## Official Cover Direction

The approved cover concept for **The Power of Knowledge** by Emmanuel Tuffet is a flat portrait, premium Power Series design with a deep navy background, metallic gold and white typography, and a luminous brain/lightbulb emerging from an open book.

### Book Identity
- Title: **THE POWER OF KNOWLEDGE**
- Subtitle: **The 9 Principles to Expand Your Mind, Master Valuable Information, and Turn What You Know into Power**
- Author: **Emmanuel Tuffet**
- Architecture: **9 Principles • 45 Lessons**
- Tagline: **LEARN DEEPLY. THINK WISELY. EXECUTE POWERFULLY.**

### Visual Language
The central visual represents illuminated intelligence, learning, comprehension, insight, and applied knowledge. Surrounding principle icons represent Knowing, Navigation, Observation, Wisdom, Learning, Experience, Discernment, Growth, and Execution. The overall style is premium, intellectual, corporate, motivational, cinematic, and commercially appealing.

# The Power of Knowledge — Manuscript

This directory is reserved for the complete manuscript of **The Power of Knowledge** by Emmanuel Tuffet.

The final manuscript will contain **9 modules and 45 lessons** built from the KNOWLEDGE framework. It should follow the Power Series professional manuscript standard: substantial connected paragraphs, smooth transitions, authoritative business and motivational voice, practical applications, evidence-aware reasoning, real-world examples, and consistent authorial voice.

## Framework

K — Knowing: Build Understanding  
N — Navigation: Know What to Learn  
O — Observation: Notice What Others Miss  
W — Wisdom: Turn Knowledge into Judgment  
L — Learning: Keep Growing  
E — Experience: Apply What You Know  
D — Discernment: Separate Truth from Noise  
G — Growth: Expand Your Mind  
E — Execution: Turn Knowledge into Results

**One Word. One Framework. One Transformation.**

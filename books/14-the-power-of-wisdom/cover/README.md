# The Power of Wisdom — Cover

## Official Cover Direction

**Title:** THE POWER OF WISDOM

**Subtitle:** The 6 Principles to Strengthen Your Judgment, Make Better Decisions, and Turn Experience into Intelligent Action

**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## Visual Identity

- Flat portrait front cover
- Premium high-end Power Series presentation
- Deep midnight navy / near-black foundation
- Metallic gold typography and accents
- Central illuminated lighthouse on a rocky peak
- Large compass as a symbol of judgment, orientation, and direction
- Six surrounding principle markers/icons representing WISDOM
- Strong executive, leadership, philosophical, and strategic identity

## Framework on Cover

- W — Worldview: See the Bigger Picture
- I — Insight: Understand Beneath the Surface
- S — Sound Judgment: Decide with Clarity
- D — Discernment: Separate Truth from Noise
- O — Observation: Learn from Patterns and Experience
- M — Maturity: Apply Knowledge Responsibly

## Tagline

**THINK DEEPLY. JUDGE WISELY. ACT RESPONSIBLY.**

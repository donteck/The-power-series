# The Power of Wisdom — Roadmap

**Subtitle:** The 6 Principles to Strengthen Your Judgment, Make Better Decisions, and Turn Experience into Intelligent Action

**Author:** Emmanuel Tuffet

## W — Worldview: See the Bigger Picture
1. Defining Wisdom in a World of Information
2. Expanding Temporal Horizons Beyond the Immediate Moment
3. Systems Thinking and Interconnected Realities
4. Cultivating Intellectual Humility and Recognizing Blind Spots
5. Mapping Your Personal Mental Models

## I — Insight: Understand Beneath the Surface
1. Distinguishing Between Data, Information, Knowledge, and Wisdom
2. Recognizing Underlying Motives and Hidden Dynamics
3. The Anatomy of Human Bias and Emotional Triggers
4. First Principles Thinking: Stripping Away Assumptions
5. Translating Surface Observations into Deeper Realities

## S — Sound Judgment: Decide with Clarity

2. Weighing Short-Term Gains Against Long-Term Consequences
3. Navigating High-Stakes Dilemmas Under Uncertainty
4. Managing Risk and Embracing Calculated Ambiguity
5. Case Study Analysis: Historical Decisions of Consequence

## D — Discernment: Separate Truth from Noise
1. Navigating the Attention Economy and Information Overload
2. Evaluating Sources, Authority, and Evidence
3. Detecting Manipulation, Fallacies, and Rhetorical Spin
4. Cultivating Internal Silence to Hear Your Own Voice
5. Practical Filtering Techniques for Daily Media Consumption

## O — Observation: Learn from Patterns and Experience
1. The Art of Active and Passive Observation
2. Extracting Lessons from Personal Failures and Successes
3. Studying Historical and Biographical Patterns
4. Recognizing Behavioral Loops in Yourself and Others
5. Maintaining a Wisdom Journal to Track Insights

## M — Maturity: Apply Knowledge Responsibly
1. The Ethical Dimension of Wise Action
2. Cultivating Empathy and Compassionate Leadership
3. Communicating Wisdom Without Arrogance
4. Mentorship and Passing Knowledge to Others
5. Integrating Wisdom into a Lifelong Practice

## Roadmaps Prompt

Build WISDOM as 6 modules: W—Worldview: see the bigger picture. I—Insight: understand beneath the surface. S—Sound Judgment: decide with clarity. D—Discernment: separate truth from noise. O—Observation: learn from patterns and experience. M—Maturity: apply knowledge responsibly.

**6 Principles • 30 Lessons**

**THINK DEEPLY. JUDGE WISELY. ACT RESPONSIBLY.**
